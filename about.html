About HomeMate Projects

A local handyman and property maintenance service built around reliable communication, useful work and a tidy result.

Based in Margaret River

A practical full-service approach to the jobs around your property

HomeMate Projects was created to give homeowners and property owners an accessible, reliable and friendly option for the repairs and maintenance jobs that can otherwise be surprisingly hard to get done.

The focus is on careful workmanship, clear communication and treating each property with respect. That applies whether the job is a door that no longer closes properly, a list of rental maintenance items, a deck repair, furniture assembly or a more involved improvement project.

Margaret River is the primary service area, with work also considered in nearby South West communities depending on the job and schedule.
